Use `ServiceCard` in the services grid. Titles are noun phrases; descriptions are one sentence that names the outcome.

```jsx
<ServiceCard title="Branding" description="Become recognized. We build the visual identity that makes you the first name your customers remember." href="/services/branding" />
<ServiceCard featured title="Personal Branding" description="The founder is the first impression." />
```

At most one `featured` tile per grid.
