Use `DeliverableCard` in the results section — concrete work, stated plainly, no adjectives.

```jsx
<DeliverableCard index="03" title="Full client-management automation" body="We connected the systems, set up HubSpot and Zoho, and automated follow-up from first meeting to closed deal." />
```

Lay them out in a 2- or 3-column grid; the top hairline is what visually separates rows.
